Thanksgiving Traditions

Thanksgiving is a time to gather with family and friends, share meals, and reflect on the things we are grateful for.

Popular traditions include preparing a Thanksgiving dinner, watching parades, and spending time with loved ones.

Favorite Holiday Foods

Thanksgiving celebrations often feature delicions dishes.

  • Roasted Turkey
  • Mashed Potatoes
  • Stuffing
  • Cranberry Sauce
  • Pumpkin Pie

Giving Thanks

Thanksgiving reminds us to appreciate the people and experiences that bring meaning to our lives.

Take a moment to express gratitude, help someone in your community, or share a meaningful memory.
