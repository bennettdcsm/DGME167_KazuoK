Acrylic Paintings

Acrylic Color & Optical Illusion Studies

This series of three acrylic studies explores foundational color theory through pointillism, geometric abstraction, and overlapping primary forms inspired by Bauhaus and Constructivist principles. Experimenting with complementary colors, optical mixing, and visual transparency, the collection demonstrates an exploration of chromatic harmony, value relationships, and compositional balance.

Target Audience: Fine arts faculty, foundation course instructors, and color theory students
