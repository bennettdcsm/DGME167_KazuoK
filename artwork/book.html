Magazine Spread & Book Covers

The Illusions of Shigeo Fukuda Magazine Spread

Designed in the editorial style of Communication Arts, the multi-page spread combines expressive asymmetrical composition with a structured three-column grid to showcase Shigeo Fukuda’s artwork while maintaining readability. The contrast between dynamic typography, carefully arranged imagery, and consistent text hierarchy demonstrates versatility in editorial layout and print design.

Target Audience: Magazine publishers, print production specialists, and editorial art directors

Life of Pi Book Cover Redesign

This cover redesign for Life of Pi explores the tension between human and beast through simplified visual storytelling, contrasting warm orange and cool blue tones, and textured paper elements that create a tactile, print-inspired aesthetic.

Target Audience: Literary fiction readers and modern book design enthusiasts

Shōgun Drop Cap Book Cover Redesign

Inspired by the themes of feudal warfare and samurai heritage in James Clavell’s Shōgun, the cover redesign combines expressive calligraphic typography, traditional Japanese symbolism, and subtle textures to balance bold martial imagery with refined historical aesthetics.

Target Audience: Historical fiction readers, publishing art directors, and jacket designers
