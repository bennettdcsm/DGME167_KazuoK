Vinyl Record Jacket

The Best of Oscar Peterson Vinyl Record Jacket

Paying tribute to jazz pianist Oscar Peterson, the vinyl jacket redesign combines a cool blue palette with bold red typography to evoke the atmosphere and visual character of mid-century jazz. Carefully selected typefaces and a refined layout balance vintage influences with contemporary design, earning the piece a place in the CSM Design Showcase exhibition.

Target Audience: Jazz collectors, music historians, and vinyl record enthusiasts
